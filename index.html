01 About the Gym

More Than a Workout.
A Standard.

Bairagarh
Bhopal, India

Iron Crush Gym is a strength-first training floor in Bairagarh, Bhopal — built around barbells, iron plates and the discipline of showing up. Whether you are chasing your first pull-up or your next personal best on the bar, this is a place to put in honest work.

Morning and evening sessions, Monday through Saturday, make it easy to fit training around a real life. Walk in, lift hard, and make consistency your standard.
